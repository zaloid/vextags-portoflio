01 / ABOUT

Production mindset.
Builder's eye.

I treat an environment as part of the game system: readable routes, strong silhouettes, consistent scale, intentional lighting and enough detail to make players remember the place without making performance an afterthought.

I have 4+ years of experience in Roblox Studio and 3+ years in Blender, with a focus on terrain, realistic construction, low / mid / high-poly work, studded styles and stylized worldbuilding.

My workflow covers environment design, object modelling, texturing, composition and optimization. I started by experimenting across multiple Roblox development disciplines, but environment work became the area where I could contribute the most to a game's identity.

I am comfortable working from a tight reference pack or helping shape the visual direction from the start. I care about scale, player flow, atmosphere and how a map reads during actual gameplay — not just in screenshots.

I regularly work long production sessions and can adapt to demanding deadlines. I have been taking commissions for 2+ years, including larger paid projects and revenue-share work.
